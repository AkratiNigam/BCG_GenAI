# BCG_GenAI Job Simulation
This repository documents my participation in the Boston Consulting Group (BCG) GenAI Virtual Experience Program hosted on Forage. The simulation provided hands-on exposure to the role of a consultant leveraging Generative AI to solve real-world business challenges.

📌 Key Highlights: 
  Business Problem Framing: Translated client challenges into structured GenAI use cases, aligning technical solutions with strategic objectives.

  Prompt Engineering: Designed effective prompts to generate high-quality outputs tailored to business needs.

  Evaluation & Risk Analysis: Assessed GenAI outputs for accuracy, bias, and ethical implications, ensuring responsible AI usage.

  Solution Design: Proposed workflows integrating GenAI into client operations for efficiency and innovation.

  Consulting Mindset: Delivered concise recommendations balancing technical rigor with business impact.

🚀 Skills Strengthened:
  Generative AI (LLMs, prompt engineering)

  Business problem translation into AI solutions

  Risk and bias evaluation in AI outputs

  Strategic communication of technical insights

  Consulting-style storytelling and recommendations

🎯 Outcome
This simulation reinforced my ability to bridge AI innovation with business strategy, preparing me to contribute to projects where GenAI drives measurable impact. It also sharpened my consulting-style communication, ensuring technical insights are translated into clear, actionable recommendations for decision-makers.
